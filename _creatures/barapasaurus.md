Barapasaurus is one of the earliest true sauropods, roughly 14 metres long, from the Early Jurassic of the Pranhita-Godavari valley. Its name means 'big-legged lizard'. Found as a bone bed of many individuals, it shows the long-necked, pillar-legged body plan already taking shape early in the age of dinosaurs.
