Indosuchus was an abelisaurid predator named from skull pieces found near Jabalpur. Its name means 'Indian crocodile', but it was a land-living, meat-eating dinosaur, not a crocodile. It was one of the first Indian theropods to be scientifically described, back in the 1930s.
