Rajasaurus is one of India's best-known dinosaurs, a stocky meat-eater about 7 to 9 metres long with a short, low horn on top of its skull. It belonged to the **abelisaurids**, the short-armed predators that ruled the southern continents while tyrannosaurs ruled the north. Its bones come from the Lameta beds of Gujarat, laid down close to the very end of the age of dinosaurs.
