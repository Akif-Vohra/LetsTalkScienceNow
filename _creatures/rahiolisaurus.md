Rahiolisaurus is a lightly built predator known from the jumbled remains of several individuals in the same Gujarat bone bed as Rajasaurus. It was a **noasaurid**, a slim, fast cousin of the bulkier abelisaurids, and shows that more than one kind of meat-eater shared this Late Cretaceous landscape.
