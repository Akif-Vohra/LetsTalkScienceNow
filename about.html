About

Hello! I am Akif.

I am fascinated by Science. I would like to raise awareness about the scientific heritage of the world, with a particular focus on the Indian subcontinent and the Asian continent, by explaining them in as simple a manner as possible. I would like to increase scientific discussions in society.

I post the things I learn so we can learn together. If you find my mistakes, please correct me! 🌻

Let's Talk Science Now is where I share those things — short visual stories, an interactive geological map of India, and deeper write-ups with sources and pathways to keep exploring.

So, let's talk science now?
