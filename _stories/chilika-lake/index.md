Chilika is a shallow **coastal lagoon** cut off from the Bay of Bengal by a barrier spit built from longshore sand and river silt — a young, Holocene landform that shifts with every storm and flood. A narrow mouth lets tides mix salt and fresh water.

That mixing makes it extraordinarily productive: a **Ramsar wetland** wintering vast flocks of migratory birds and sheltering the rare Irrawaddy dolphin.
