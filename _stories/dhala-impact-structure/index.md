Dhala is the **largest confirmed meteorite-impact structure in India** — and among the oldest known anywhere. It sits in the ancient granites of the **Bundelkhand craton**, where **shocked quartz** and **impact-melt breccia** record the moment a large body slammed into the crust and briefly melted it.

The impact is **Paleoproterozoic**, its exact age still debated (roughly 1.7–2.5 billion years). That makes it far older and larger than the young Lonar crater, though hundreds of millions of years of erosion have stripped away the rim and floor, leaving a broad ring of disturbed and once-molten rock rather than a bowl.
