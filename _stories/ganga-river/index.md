The Ganga gathers from Himalayan glaciers above Gangotri, joins its headstreams at Devprayag, spills onto the plains at Haridwar, and then flows some 2,500 km across the **Indo-Gangetic Plain** to the Bay of Bengal.

Its enormous sediment load — shed from the rising Himalaya — built, together with the Brahmaputra, the world's largest delta (the Sundarbans). This outline covers the Indian reach; downstream the river continues through Bangladesh as the Padma.
