Gangotri is one of the **largest glaciers in the Himalaya** — roughly 30 km long, fed by peaks approaching 7,000 m in the Garhwal Himalaya. Its snout, **Gaumukh** ("cow's mouth"), is the source of the **Bhagirathi**, the headstream that joins the Alaknanda to become the [Ganga](/stories/ganga/).

Like most Himalayan glaciers, Gangotri is **retreating**: its snout has pulled back well over a kilometre over the past century. Because glaciers like this feed the great Himalayan rivers through the dry season, that retreat is a closely watched signal of a warming climate — and a long-term concern for the hundreds of millions downstream.
