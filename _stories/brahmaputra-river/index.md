Rising on the Tibetan plateau as the Yarlung Tsangpo, the Brahmaputra swings around the **Eastern Himalayan Syntaxis** and enters Assam, where it becomes a vast **braided** river shifting across a wide floodplain.

Its seasonal floods build and erase islands — including Majuli — and its silt, with the Ganga's, feeds the Sundarbans delta. This outline covers the Assam reach.
