The Narmada flows west to the Arabian Sea along a **down-faulted trough** between the Vindhya and Satpura ranges, following the deep **Son–Narmada lineament** — one of the oldest and most persistent fracture lines in the Indian crust.

Because it runs in a structural rift rather than a self-cut valley, the river drops little and floods broad terraces — sediments that have yielded Deccan-era dinosaur eggs and one of India's few archaic human fossils, at Hathnora.
