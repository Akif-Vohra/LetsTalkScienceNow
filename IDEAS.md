## From beta testers / followers

_A follower tried the geological map (Aug 2026) — loved the smooth experience, the
river routes, and the terrain outlines. Their suggestions:_

- **Photos for more pins.** Many sites still show no picture. Extend the pin photo galleries
  to cover them. _(Done: all mountain pins + East India + Northeast. Still open: plateaus,
  rivers, lakes, coasts, deserts, cratons/basins, impact craters, mining sites.)_
- **"Fun fact" snippets.** Add little bite-sized facts into the feature write-ups; people
  love those small nuggets of information.
- **Spell out `Ma` / `Ga`.** Show what the units mean — `Ma` = *megaannum* (millions of
  years ago), `Ga` = *gigaannum* (billions of years ago) — e.g. a tooltip on the fact-card
  age, or a one-line legend near the time slider.
